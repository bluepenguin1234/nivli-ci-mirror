How it works

1

Choose the apps that wait

Pick the apps, categories and sites you would rather earn. Nivli holds them back with Apple's Screen Time settings, on this iPhone, for you.

2

Move

Log a workout by hand, or connect Apple Health and let a run, a lift or a class unlock the day on its own. You set the minimum length.

3

Then scroll

The apps open for the rest of the day and the streak goes up one. At midnight they lock again. Rest days are yours to set, and you can take today off.

Nivli cannot close an app you already have open. iOS shows the Nivli lock screen the next time you open it.

Private by design

Nivli has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code; the only network traffic is Apple's own App Store, for the subscription. The apps you choose are handed to Nivli as opaque tokens, so Nivli can shield them without ever learning their names. If you connect Apple Health, Nivli reads your workouts on the iPhone and writes nothing back; that data never leaves the device. Your workout log, streak and settings live in Nivli's own storage on this iPhone and in your own device backup. Turn Nivli off or delete it and iOS lifts the shields. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
