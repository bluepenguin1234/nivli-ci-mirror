Nivli 3.0

Move first. Then scroll.

16 September 2026 Version 3.0.0 iPhone, iOS 18 and later $2.99 a month

Nivli 3.0 is a new product in the same App Store listing. It locks the apps you choose until you have worked out today, then opens them until midnight.

Everything is built: eight onboarding pages, the paywall, Home, the log sheet, Settings, the lock screen iOS draws over a blocked app, and the piece that re-locks everything at midnight.

The code is written, reviewed and pushed. It has not been compiled on a Mac yet, because this machine cannot run one. That first compile is step one of your list.

What Nivli does

Five sentences cover the whole product.

  • You pick the apps that wait. Single apps, whole categories, or websites, chosen in Apple's own picker. iOS holds them shut for you.
  • A workout opens them. Log one by hand in two taps, or connect Apple Health and let a run, a lift or a class do it without you opening Nivli.
  • You set what counts. Any workout at or over your minimum unlocks the day. The minimum starts at 20 minutes and can be 10 to 60.
  • At midnight it all locks again and the streak carries on. Seven dots on Home show the week behind you, and your longest run is kept.
  • Rest days are yours. Pick weekdays you always rest, or take today off in one tap. Nothing is locked and the streak is not broken.

How a day works

The same five beats, every day, whether or not the app is ever opened.

1

Midnight

A small background piece of Nivli puts the shields back on the apps you chose. Nothing is tapped and nothing is shown.

2

You open a blocked app

iOS shows the Nivli shield instead of the app. Dark, the mark, and two words: Move first.

3

You work out

Any kind, anywhere. It only has to be as long as your minimum, which is 20 minutes unless you change it.

4

Log it, or Health tells Nivli

Two taps in the app. Or an Apple Watch run reports itself and the apps open while the phone is still in your pocket.

5

Open until midnight

The ring fills, the streak goes up one, and the apps stay open for the rest of the day.

The only two Nivli ever sends

The two notifications

Both are made and delivered on the phone. There is no server anywhere that could send a third.

What each one is

  • Apps unlocked"Workout detected. Streak: 5 days." Sent only when Apple Health finds a qualifying workout while Nivli is closed, so the person knows their apps opened without doing anything.
  • Still locked"Your apps are waiting. Move, log it, and they open." At the evening time they chose, and only on a day that is not done. Once today is unlocked or is a rest day, tonight's nudge is cancelled and the next one is tomorrow's.

Both are off unless the person turned reminders on, and there is nothing else. No streak warnings, no marketing, no re-engagement.

The rules

Every screen and both background pieces obey the same short list.

RuleHow it works
What unlocks Any one workout that is at or over the minimum. Manual or from Apple Health, it makes no difference. The kind makes no difference either.
Minimum minutes 20 by default. Can be 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60, changed at any time in Settings. A shorter workout is kept in the history but does not unlock anything, and the log sheet says so before it is saved.
Midnight The phone's own local day, midnight to midnight. At 00:00 the apps lock again, done by a background piece of Nivli, with the app closed. If iOS ever refuses to run it, the app re-applies the locks the next time it is opened.
Rest days Weekdays chosen in Settings. Nothing is locked on them. The streak is neither grown nor broken. None are set on a new install.
Take today off One tap, with a confirmation, applied to today only. Same effect as a weekly rest day. There is no limit on how often it can be used, on purpose: a lock the person cannot lift is a lock Apple would reject.
Streak Consecutive days ending today in which every day is either a workout day or a rest day. Only workout days are counted. Today is never counted as a miss while it is still open. The longest run ever is kept separately and never goes down.
Subscription lapse Nothing is locked, ever. Home shows a card: "Your subscription ended. Nothing is locked. Start again to keep your streak going." The history and the streak are untouched and come straight back on resubscribing.
Deleting the app Every lock comes off immediately. That is iOS behaviour, not a Nivli setting, and it cannot be prevented. The workout history goes with the app.
What Nivli can see Whether something is selected, how many things, the length and kind of each workout, the streak, and the settings. All of it on this phone only.
What Nivli cannot see Which apps were picked. iOS hands over sealed tokens and draws the names itself. Nivli also cannot see anything inside those apps, cannot close an app that is already open, and cannot touch any other device or person.

Business

One price, one product, no infrastructure.

The offer

$2.99 a month, and nothing else

  • One subscription, monthly, $2.99 in the United States. Apple's storefront sets the price everywhere else.
  • A hard paywall at the end of onboarding. There is no free tier and no trial today.
  • A seven day free trial can be switched on later. It is an App Store Connect change only, no new build, and the paywall picks it up on its own.
  • Nothing else is for sale. No ads, no consumables, no upsell inside the app.
What is reused

The existing product and listing

  • Same App Store record, same bundle, released as version 3.0.0. The old product's listing copy is fully replaced.
  • The existing $2.99 monthly product is reused as is, so there is nothing to create and nothing to price.
  • The old yearly and lifetime products are removed from sale but not deleted. Anyone who bought one keeps full access in 3.0 through Restore Purchases.
Running cost

No server, no account

  • Nothing runs anywhere but the customer's iPhone. There is no database, no hosting bill and no scaling risk.
  • No sign up and no password, so there is no support load from logins and no breach to have.
  • The only ongoing cost is the Apple Developer Program, which is already paid.
Support

Three flat web pages

  • A landing page, a privacy policy and a support page, hosted free on GitHub Pages at the addresses already built into the app.
  • They replace the old product's pages and have to go live before the app is submitted.

Privacy and Apple compliance

The parts a reviewer will look at, and the answers that are already written down.

  • Nothing is collected. No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third party code at all. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected", and the privacy manifests in all three parts of the app say the same.
  • The app list never leaves iOS. Apple's picker hands back sealed tokens. Nivli stores them and asks iOS to draw them. Even the app's own code cannot read an app name out of one.
  • Apple Health is read only and optional. Nivli reads workouts and nothing else, never writes anything back, and works fully without it. The permission text explains this, and the privacy policy names Health.
  • Screen Time is used on the person's own phone. It is requested as individual authorization, for the person using the device, on that device. There are no parental, family or remote features, which is exactly what Apple's entitlement request asks about.
  • The subscription meets Apple's rules. Price, period, Restore Purchases, the standard licence link, a privacy policy link and the renewal paragraph are all on the paywall, with the price read live from the App Store.
  • Nivli always fails open. No subscription, unfinished onboarding, nothing chosen, a rest day or a workout logged all mean nothing is locked. Anything unusual leaves the phone alone, which is both the safe behaviour and the one Apple expects.

What was verified and what was not

Being straight about this now is cheaper than being surprised later.

Verified

What passes today

  • The repository's own packaging checks pass. They read every file list, plist, icon, privacy manifest, entitlement and product ID and confirm they agree with each other and with the build pipeline.
  • Every Swift file was parsed for syntax on this Windows machine, so there are no unbalanced braces, stray characters or broken files.
  • Unit tests are written for the parts that carry the rules: the day boundary, the streak with rest days and gaps, the lock decision and the order it checks things in, the saved state round trip, the Health import and its duplicate handling, and the subscription to entitlement mapping.
  • Every screen, every button and every piece of copy has been read back against the design spec, which is what this document is written from.
Not verified

What is still open

  • It has not been compiled on a Mac. The code was written against Apple's published interfaces from documentation, not against a compiler. Expect one or two rounds of small fixes on the first cloud build. This is normal and it is quick.
  • Nothing about Screen Time can be tested anywhere but a real iPhone. The simulator has no Screen Time at all, so the shield, the picker and the midnight re-lock are only proved on your device.
  • The Apple Health background unlock needs real hardware, an Apple Watch or the Fitness app, to prove that a workout opens the apps without Nivli being opened.
  • The price string comes from the App Store at run time, so it is only confirmed once a build is on a device signed in to a real account.
  • The first compile is step 1 of your list below, and it is free.

What you need to do

Nine steps, in order. None of them needs a Mac, and none of them needs any code. Each says what it costs you in time.

  1. Compile it on Codemagic15 minutes, free

    Sign in to Codemagic with GitHub, add the nivli repository, and start the "iOS Compile" workflow on main. Green means we are in good shape. Red means copying the first error line back to the engineering session for a fix. The free plan gives 500 build minutes a month and this uses about 8. Then run the unit test workflow the same way.

  2. Ask Apple for the Family Controls entitlement10 minutes, then days to weeksThe long pole

    This is the one thing on the list with a wait you do not control, so submit it today, before anything else. Screen Time blocking needs a permission Apple grants per app before it can be shipped, and without it the signed build fails at signing. The form is one page and the description to paste is already written in the handoff document, covering all three parts of the app. Everything else can proceed while you wait.

  3. Switch on the capabilities in the Apple Developer portal10 minutes, after step 2

    Once Apple approves, tick App Groups, HealthKit and Family Controls on the main app, untick iCloud and Push Notifications, and register the two small extension identifiers with the same settings. The exact clicks are written out in the build document.

  4. App Store Connect: the subscription and the agreement15 minutes

    Check that the Paid Apps agreement is still active. Rename the monthly product's display name to "Nivli Monthly" with the description "Unlock your apps by moving first", confirm $2.99 a month, and add a review screenshot. Remove the yearly and lifetime products from sale without deleting them, so existing customers keep their access.

  5. Codemagic credentials5 minutes, only if needed

    The credentials from the previous Nivli work unchanged. Only if Codemagic was set up from scratch do you need a fresh App Store Connect API key and the signing certificate file from your Desktop.

  6. TestFlight build and device test30 minutes, after steps 1 to 5

    Run the release workflow, install it on your iPhone through TestFlight, and walk the device test list. The three that matter most: a picked app shows the "Move first." shield, logging a 30 minute workout opens it, and the next morning it is locked again. Anything wrong goes back to the engineering session with the step number.

  7. Publish the web pages5 minutes, the day you submit

    The app links to a privacy page and a support page that currently serve the old product. Fill your legal name into the three new files, then upload them to the GitHub Pages repository. They go live within a minute.

  8. App Store listing and submission45 minutes

    Create version 3.0.0 and fill it in. Every field has a source file already written: name and description, what is new, screenshots, the privacy answers and the review notes. Delete every old screenshot first, because the reviewer compares them to the app and this is a different product. Then submit for review.

  9. After approvalOngoing

    Watch the crash reports for the first week. Optionally switch on a seven day free trial, which needs no new build. Optionally enable a second compile check on GitHub as a backup to Codemagic.

Decisions made for you

You were not available while this was designed, so every open question was answered rather than left hanging. Any of these can be changed by saying so.

  • Same app record, version 3.0.0. Nivli 3.0 replaces the memory app in the same App Store listing rather than being a new app, so the existing subscription, agreement and customers carry over.
  • The existing $2.99 monthly product is reused. Yearly and lifetime are retired from sale but honoured for anyone who already bought them.
  • A hard paywall after onboarding, with no free tier. One price, one decision.
  • Manual logs are an honor system. A workout counts at 20 minutes by default, adjustable from 10 to 60 in Settings. The sheet says this in as many words.
  • Weekly rest days and "take today off" both exist and neither one breaks the streak.
  • The app fails open on purpose. If the subscription lapses, onboarding is unfinished, or nothing is picked, nothing is ever locked. Apple would reject anything else.
  • iOS 18 and later, iPhone only, dark by default with a light option and a match iPhone option.
  • No widgets, no Siri, no Watch app and no sharing in 3.0. They were considered and left out to keep the first release small.
