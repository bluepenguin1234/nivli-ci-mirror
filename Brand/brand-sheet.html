Nivli brand sheet

The mark, the icon, the colours, the type and the app's building blocks in both looks, with the rules that keep them consistent. Remember when.

the mark

“Ni”: one N stroke, completed by a mint dot

The right leg of the N stops short; the dot finishes it into an “i”. The dot is the moment something happened, the thing Nivli keeps for you. Stroke width is 92 on the 1024 grid with round caps, dot radius 50, and the overdue ring sits at radius 82. Nothing else is ever added to it.

  • Clear space: keep at least one dot-diameter (100 grid units) of empty space around the mark.
  • Smallest size: 16 px tall on screen; below that use the dot alone as a bullet, never a shrunken N.
  • Colour: white on ink or dark grounds, ink on white or paper. The dot is always mint.
the app icon

Ink square, white N, mint dot

The store and iOS apply their own corner mask, so the source icon is a plain square. The rounded version is for the website, documents and anywhere outside Apple's grid.

colour

Ink, mint, and one deeper teal for light screens

Mint is too light to read on white, so the in-app accent is teal in the Light look and mint in the Dark look. Orange is reserved for “overdue”; it is a state, not a brand colour.

type

The system face, so the app reads like the iPhone

No custom fonts. Titles and elapsed times use the rounded design; body text uses the standard text face with Dynamic Type. On this page the browser substitutes its own system font, so shapes differ slightly from the iPhone.

voice

How Nivli talks

  • Short and human: “Last: 3 months ago”, “Did it”, “Tomorrow”.
  • States the resolved date and time: “set a reminder for Dec 12, 2026 at 9:00 AM”.
  • Says plainly when something isn't saved; never guesses or invents.
  • Sentence case. No exclamation marks. No “task”, “to-do” or “productivity”.
  • Buttons say exactly what happens: Log it, Save detail, Save all, Share details.

Do and don't

  • Do use the SVGs for marketing and the PNGs for apps and documents.
  • Do keep mint as the one highlight, on ink or near-black.
  • Don't add gradients, shadows, badges, text or a second mark to the icon.
  • Don't recolour the dot, stretch the N, or put the mark on busy photos.
  • Don't set mint body text on white; use teal.
